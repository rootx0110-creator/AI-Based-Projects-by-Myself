=====================================================================
 HOW TO CHECK YOUR OWN MOBILE ARTIFACTS (STEP BY STEP)
 Mobile Device Forensic Workflow (MFW) - Android devices only
=====================================================================

SHORT ANSWER
------------
YES. Connect your own Android phone by USB and the app can extract
and show you:

  - Call logs     (incoming / outgoing / missed, duration)
  - SMS messages  (address, direction, body)
  - Contacts      (names + numbers)
  - Installed apps (package inventory, 3rd-party + system)
  - Screenshots   (whatever is on the phone screen right now)
  - Device info   (model, Android version, security patch level)

Everything is saved under  data\cases\<YOUR_CASE>\  and you can
download a full HTML report of the findings with one click.

NOTE: this is a LOGICAL extraction tool. It reads user-level data
through Android's own backup/inventory mechanisms. It does NOT root
the phone, does NOT bypass locks, and cannot read encrypted app
databases (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.). iPhone is not supported.


=====================================================================
PART 1 - PREPARE YOUR PHONE (one-time setup)
=====================================================================

STEP 1. Enable Developer Options
        - Open  Settings > About phone
        - Find  Build number  (sometimes under  Software information)
        - Tap it 7 times quickly
        - You will see "You are now a developer!"

STEP 2. Enable USB Debugging
        - Settings > System > Developer options
          (on some phones: Settings > Additional settings)
        - Turn ON  "USB debugging"

STEP 3. Install ADB on the PC (the app needs adb.exe)
        Easiest: download "SDK Platform Tools" from Google:
          https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools
        - Unzip it to  C:\platform-tools\
          (the app automatically finds  C:\platform-tools\adb.exe)
        - Or unzip anywhere and add that folder to your PATH
        Check: the app header should show "ADB ready" on next launch.

STEP 4. (Recommended) Turn the screen ON and UNLOCK the phone.
        A locked screen can block the backup confirmation dialog.


=====================================================================
PART 2 - RUN THE WORKFLOW IN THE APP
=====================================================================

STEP 5. Start the app
        - Double-click  MobileForensicWorkflow.exe
        - First run creates a sample case DEMO-001 automatically.

STEP 6. Create YOUR case
        - Left menu > "Case Manager"
        - Fill: Case number (e.g. 2026-MY-001), Title, Examiner
          (your name), Agency (optional)
        - Click  "+ Create case"
        - It becomes the ACTIVE case (see the chip in the header).

STEP 7. Connect the phone
        - Plug the USB cable into the PC
        - On the phone, set USB mode to "File transfer / MTP"
          (pure "charging only" often blocks ADB)
        - Go to "Extraction" in the left menu
        - Click  "Refresh devices"
        - FIRST TIME ONLY: the phone shows
          "Allow USB debugging? (RSA fingerprint)"
          -> tick "Always allow from this computer" -> tap ALLOW

STEP 8. Read device info (sanity check)
        - Select your device row in the device table
        - Click  "Read device info"
        - The log shows model, Android version, security patch etc.
        If this works, ADB is fully functional.

STEP 9. Choose an extraction method and start
        A) "ADB Backup (logical acquisition)"  <-- the main one
           - Click  "Start extraction"
           - WATCH YOUR PHONE: Android shows
             "Back up my data - Do you want to back up...?"
             -> tap  "BACK UP MY DATA"  (do NOT tap Cancel)
           - The tool waits up to ~4 minutes
           - When done, the log shows the unpack + parsing results
             and the evidence file gets SHA-256 hashed
        B) "Package inventory" - quick list of installed apps
        C) "Screenshot capture" - PNG of the current phone screen
        D) "Demo dataset" - no phone needed (offline demo data)

STEP 10. Review the artifacts
        - Left menu > "Artifacts"
        - Pick type: calls / sms / contacts / apps
        - Use the search box to filter (number, name, text...)
        - Optional: "Export CSV" to save the current table

STEP 11. Download the HTML report
        - Left menu > "Reports"
        - Fill report title / classification / summary
        - Click  "DOWNLOAD HTML REPORT"
        - Choose where to save (defaults to the case reports folder)
        - Open the .html file in any browser - it contains case info,
          device info, methodology, findings tables, SHA-256 hashes
          of every evidence file, and the chain of custody.
        - "Open last report" re-opens the most recent one.


=====================================================================
WHERE MY DATA IS STORED (all local, next to the exe)
=====================================================================
  data\cases_index.json                  list of your cases
  data\audit.log                         global action trail
  data\cases\<CASE_NUMBER>\
      audit.txt                          per-case action trail
      chain_of_custody.csv               timestamps + SHA-256 hashes
      evidence\                          raw acquisition files
                                         (backup_<case>.ab, PNGs, ...)
      extracted\                         unpacked data + artifacts.json
      reports\                           your downloaded HTML reports
      exports\                           CSV exports

You can copy the whole case folder to a USB drive as evidence.


=====================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING (self-examination specifics)
=====================================================================

Q: "Refresh devices" shows nothing / list is empty
   A: - Check the USB cable (some cables are charge-only - try another)
      - Phone: pull down the USB notification -> switch to
        "File transfer / MTP"
      - Revoke + re-authorize: Developer options > Revoke USB
        debugging authorizations, then re-plug and accept the RSA prompt
      - Check "adb ready" chip in the app header (see Part 1 Step 3)

Q: Device listed as "unauthorized"
   A: Unlock the phone and accept the "Allow USB debugging?" dialog.

Q: Backup finished but artifacts are empty
   A: - The phone's backup transport may be restricted or disabled
        by the manufacturer (some newer Android builds restrict
        'adb backup'). Check the extraction log for warnings.
      - Screen was locked so the confirmation never got tapped ->
        unlock the screen, run again, and tap "BACK UP MY DATA".
      - Your phone may have no data in those providers
        (check with the "Demo dataset" method to confirm the app
        pipeline itself works).

Q: "adb backup" seems to hang
   A: It is waiting for the on-device confirmation dialog. Look at
      the phone, tap "BACK UP MY DATA". Timeout is ~4 minutes.

Q: The phone screen looks odd after screenshot capture
   A: It is just a screenshot - nothing on the phone was modified.

Q: Samsung / Xiaomi / etc. show "backup cancelled"
   A: Some vendor ROMs disable the backup transport. The package
      inventory and screenshot methods still work on those devices.

PRIVACY & LEGAL
---------------
Extracting a device you do not own or lack authorization to examine
may be illegal. Use this tool only on your own devices or within an
authorized investigation. All processing stays on your PC; nothing
is uploaded anywhere.
