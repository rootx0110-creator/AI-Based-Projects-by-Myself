ABOUT THE ENGINE

How the pipeline works

01
Lex & Tokenize

Your sentence is normalized, split into clauses and scanned for known security vocabulary.

02
Artifact Extraction

IOCs and indicators are pulled out: IPs, domains, URLs, hashes, processes, file paths, registry keys, ports, usernames and more.

03
Concept Detection

Intent is classified into detection concepts — download-and-execute, credential dumping, persistence, C2 beaconing — using a curated lexicon.

04
Sigma Generation

A standard Sigma YAML rule is assembled: logsource, selection, condition, false positives and MITRE tags.

05
YARA Generation

A YARA rule is built from the same understanding with meta, strings and a match condition.

06
Report & Download

A self-contained, styled HTML report packages everything for paste-into-your-SIEM or sharing with the team.

DISCLAIMER

This tool is designed for legal, defensive, and lab/authorized use only. Generated rules are heuristic drafts — always validate them against your environment's log pipelines before production use.
