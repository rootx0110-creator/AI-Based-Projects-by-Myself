    About FRAMC

    Architecture, methodology and limitations.

    What it checks

    • Shadowed rules — later rules unreachable behind a superset
    • Ordering overrides — ALLOW before covering DENY (critical)
    • World-open ANY/ANY permits
    • Sensitive-port exposure (SSH, RDP, SQL…)
    • Duplicates & dead rules, missing default-deny
    • Invalid CIDR / notation, non-logged allows

    Supported formats

      How posture is graded

      Posture starts at 100 and is reduced per finding severity (CRITICAL −18, HIGH −10, MEDIUM −5, LOW −2, INFO −0.5) with rewards for default-deny and full logging.
